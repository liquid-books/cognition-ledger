# The Cognition Ledger Manifesto

:::{figure} ../images/ch00-power-to-prize.png
:label: fig-ch00-power-to-prize
:alt: A three-step staircase from buy the new power to redesign the work to decide what the power is for, with the money sitting only on the top step
:width: 100%
:align: center

Buying new power is only the first step. The money arrives once the work is redesigned and someone decides what the power is for.
:::

Every great shift in how work gets done has hidden its prize from the people who paid for it. The engine was bought long before the factory was redesigned, and the money arrived only when someone decided what the new power was for. We are buying thinking machines now, and we refuse to wait forty years to find the money.

:::{figure} ../images/ch00-forty-year-wait.png
:label: fig-ch00-forty-year-wait
:alt: Two timelines, the old way with forty years of waiting between buying the engine and the money arriving, and our way where deciding what thinking machines are for brings the money soon after purchase
:width: 100%
:align: center

The engine paid off only after decades of waiting. With thinking machines, the decision about what they are for, not the purchase, sets the date the money arrives.
:::

Through this work we have come to value:

:::{figure} ../images/ch00-how-to-read.png
:label: fig-ch00-how-to-read
:alt: A worked example of banked hours over saved hours, with the left side labelled what turns up on the P&L, the right side labelled still has value, and an arrow saying when they compete, choose the left
:width: 100%
:align: center

How to read each value statement. The right side still has value; when the two compete, the left side wins because it is what reaches the P&L.
:::

**Banked hours** over saved hours.

:::{figure} ../images/ch00-saved-vs-banked.png
:label: fig-ch00-saved-vs-banked
:alt: Two cards, a saved hour with four empty checkboxes and a banked hour with job, owner, date and line on the books all ticked
:width: 100%
:align: center

A saved hour is only a possibility. A banked hour has a job, an owner, a date and a line on the books.
:::

**Redesigned work** over automated tasks.

:::{figure} ../images/ch00-task-vs-stream.png
:label: fig-ch00-task-vs-stream
:alt: Two process streams, one with a single faster step but long waiting gaps still between every step, and one redesigned with the waiting removed so the whole stream finishes much sooner
:width: 100%
:align: center

Automating one task speeds up one step while the waiting stays. Redesigning the work removes the waiting, so the whole stream gets faster.
:::

**Verified value** over projected value.

:::{figure} ../images/ch00-promise-vs-signature.png
:label: fig-ch00-promise-vs-signature
:alt: A projected value card with a dashed forecast line labelled promise, an arrow labelled measured on real work, and a verified value card with a signed ledger labelled the CFO signs it
:width: 100%
:align: center

A projection is a promise. Measured on real work, it becomes a verified number the CFO will sign.
:::

**Decisions** over tools.

:::{figure} ../images/ch00-time-decides.png
:label: fig-ch00-time-decides
:alt: A tool produces freed time, which splits into two paths, people decide and the time has a destination, or nobody decides and the time decides for itself
:width: 100%
:align: center

A tool frees time but decides nothing. Either people decide where the time goes, or the time decides for them.
:::

**The things on the right have value. The things on the left are what turn up on the P&L.**

:::{figure} ../images/ch00-slide-or-ledger.png
:label: fig-ch00-slide-or-ledger
:alt: Two containers, a vendor slide holding saved hours, automated tasks, projected value and tools, and a P&L ledger holding banked hours, redesigned work, verified value and decisions
:width: 100%
:align: center

The right-hand values fill vendor slides; the left-hand values fill the P&L. Ask which document your AI value lives in.
:::

A saved hour is a possibility; a banked hour has a job, an owner, a date and a line on the books. An automated task makes one step faster; redesigned work makes the whole stream faster, because the waiting goes too. A projection is a promise; a verified number is one the CFO will sign. And a tool, however clever, decides nothing. People decide where the time goes, or the time decides for them.
