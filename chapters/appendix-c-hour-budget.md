# Appendix C: The Hour Budget Template

:::{figure} ../images/appC-infographic.png
:label: fig-appC-infographic
:alt: Opening infographic summarising The Hour Budget Template
:width: 80%
:align: center

*Every freed hour gets a lever, a P&L line, an owner and a date.*
:::


The Hour Budget is the plan that gives every freed hour a destination. It is drafted before anything is built, signed by the budget owner whose line will change, and checked by finance every quarter. Any hour without a destination is shown as Leak and valued at \$0, because nothing on the P&L changes.

## The Six Levers

Every freed hour goes to exactly one lever. Each lever lands on a specific P&L line, carries a value class, needs a specific kind of evidence, and is priced its own way.

| Lever | What happens to the hours | P&L line | Value class | Evidence finance accepts | How to price it |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Cut** | Stop paying for something: overtime, temps, contractors, an outsourced service | Operating expense: overtime, contract labour, outside services | Hard savings | Canceled purchase order or contract; overtime report against the same months last year | The rate of what you stop paying for |
| **Avoid** | Absorb growth or attrition without the hires the plan called for | Personnel expense against budget | Cost avoidance | Approved headcount plan with the requisition removed; HR system shows no backfill | Loaded hourly rate of the role not hired |
| **Grow** | Move hours to revenue work: selling, retention, new offers | Revenue and gross margin | Revenue attributable | CRM attribution of pipeline or bookings to the redeployed work, against a control group | Gross margin earned per redeployed hour |
| **Fix** | Fewer errors, credits, write-offs and penalties | Cost of goods sold, credits and allowances, bad debt, penalties | Hard savings | Error log, credit-memo totals or write-off report against the baseline | Cost of poor quality avoided |
| **Speed** | Shorter lead time turns into cash or capacity sooner | Interest expense and working capital | Cash | Days sales outstanding or lead-time report; treasury's cost of capital | Cash freed × cost of capital |
| **Invest** | Hours go to a funded strategic project with its own business case | That project's own business case | Strategic | Project charter with owner, value case and dates | Booked in that project's case, not here |
| **Leak** | Not yet assigned | None | None | Reported every quarter | \$0 |

Fix and Speed don't come from hours at all. Fix banks errors that no longer happen, and Speed banks the value of cash arriving sooner. That is why a portfolio's projected banked value can be larger than its theoretical value.

## The Banking Rules

1. **Decide before you build.** A draft Hour Budget is the gate into Building.
2. **The budget owner signs.** The person whose budget line changes signs, not the project team.
3. **One hour, one destination.** Hours assigned plus Leak equal net hours freed, never more.
4. **Name the date.** Each line has a realization date and the quarter in which finance will look for it.
5. **Price it like finance would.** Cut uses the rate you stop paying; Avoid uses the loaded rate of the hire not made; Speed uses the cost of capital, not the loaded rate.
6. **Show the leak.** Unassigned hours are reported every quarter until they are assigned or the idea is re-scoped.

## Worked Example: Brightwater OTC-01, the Quote Builder Skill

The quote Skill frees 10,246 hours a year. The role's loaded hourly rate is \$42.87, so the theoretical value is \$439,270. Here is where the hours went.

| # | Lever | What exactly happens | Hours/yr | Rate | Projected \$/yr | P&L line | Evidence | Owner | Quarter |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Avoid | Four inside-sales hires in the plan are not made; growth is absorbed by the current team (4 × 1,880 hours) | 7,520 | \$42.87/hr | \$322,382 | Personnel expense vs budget | Requisitions removed from the approved plan | Value stream owner | Q1 |
| 2 | Grow | Proactive calls to lapsed accounts | 2,000 | \$35/hr gross margin | \$70,000 | Revenue and gross margin | CRM attribution vs a control territory | Value stream owner | Q2 |
| | Leak | Not yet assigned | 726 | | \$0 | | | | |
| | **Total** | | **10,246** | | **\$392,382** | | | | |

The banked plan (\$392,382) is smaller than the theoretical value (\$439,270), and only the Avoid line has hard evidence behind it so far. That is an honest number, and an honest number is the one a CFO will defend in a board meeting.

- Hours assigned plus Leak equal net hours freed.
- Every Cut and Avoid line names the actual contract, purchase order or requisition.
- Every Grow line names its control group.
- Every Speed line uses the cost of capital, not the loaded rate.
- You have written the two sentences you will say to the people whose work changes.
