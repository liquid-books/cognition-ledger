# The Cognition Ledger
## Why AI Saves Time, Not Money, and How Leaders Bank the Difference

**By Dr. Ernesto Lee and Alex Schwartz**

---

Two hundred people each save four hours a week with AI. The slide says \$1.5 million. Next year's P&L doesn't move a dollar. AI didn't fail. Nobody gave the hours a job.

*The Cognition Ledger* is the management method for the age of cheap thinking. It shows you how to find where AI time actually goes, decide what each freed hour is for before you build anything, and prove the result in a number your CFO will sign.

::::{grid} 1 1 3 3
:gutter: 3

:::{grid-item-card} 🧭 Six moves
Map, Sort, Size, Ship, Bank, Prove: one path from a saved hour to a banked dollar.
:::

:::{grid-item-card} 📒 One ledger
Theoretical, projected and realized value, kept side by side and never added together.
:::

:::{grid-item-card} 👥 Four readers
Written for CEOs, CFOs, managers and data scientists. No technical background required.
:::
::::

## What you'll be able to do

- See your office as a value stream and find the waiting that hides inside every process.
- Choose between plain automation, a Skill, an agent or a person, using the simplest thing that works.
- Give every freed hour a job through the Hour Budget, so it lands on a line of the P&L.
- Run the ledger as a living board, lead the people conversation honestly, and price outcomes instead of hours.
